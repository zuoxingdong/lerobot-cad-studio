LeKiwi camera mounts (overhead mast + wrist arch) - print order
===============================================================
File: lekiwi_camera_mounts.3mf   (5 objects, millimetres, all pre-oriented on z=0)
The same five plates are also single STLs, in ../corner_mast_printed/print/ and
../df200_arch/print/, if you prefer them. Those files keep short names (spar_lower_PRINT.stl);
the 3mf objects carry the longer names listed below, which is what the studio exports.

MATERIAL
  PLA. Any colour. 0.4 mm nozzle.
  ~291 g of part, plus support and brim, so budget ~380 g.

APPLIES TO EVERY PART
  - Print in the orientation supplied. Please do NOT auto-orient or re-orient. Every object is
    already sitting the correct way up on z=0, and the orientations were chosen by measuring
    flat first-layer area, not by eye: one of these parts used to be supplied balanced on a
    point with 0.00 mm2 of bed contact, which is exactly the mistake this pack avoids.
  - Please do NOT run mesh repair / "fix errors" on any file. All five meshes are watertight,
    manifold, winding-consistent and single-bodied as supplied.
  - Supports, where asked for, are "TOUCHING BUILD PLATE ONLY", never "everywhere".

PER PART - only the supports and the brim really matter

  1-spar_lower ....... NO supports. NO brim (2098 mm2 of flat first layer, it is not going
                       anywhere). 248.0 mm tall, so it needs 250 mm of Z or more - on a
                       250 mm machine that is 2.0 mm to spare, so check the gantry.
                       >>> This part is HOLLOW, with the camera cable duct running its full
                       length. Support generated inside it cannot be removed and the part is
                       scrap. If your slicer offers "support on build plate only", that is the
                       setting; if it wants to support everywhere, turn supports OFF for this
                       object - nothing on it needs them (1.0 % overhang).

  2-spar_upper ....... Supports TOUCHING BUILD PLATE ONLY, plus a brim of about 8 mm.
                       229.6 mm tall on 337 mm2 of first layer, so the brim is what keeps it
                       on the plate. 2.7 % overhang, and all of it is in the last 60 mm: the
                       two hinge cheeks and the index fan at the very top. Nothing lower down
                       needs support. Also hollow - same rule as part 1.

  3-head_MAST and
  5-head_WRIST-ARCH .. THE SAME PART, printed twice - one goes on the overhead mast and one on
                       the wrist arch. They are identical geometry; they are listed separately
                       only so the quantity is unambiguous.
                       Supports TOUCHING BUILD PLATE ONLY. The two hinge-lug ends are a 23.7 mm
                       bridge 5-8 mm off the plate and they do need support under them. No brim
                       (976 mm2 of first layer). 17.5 mm tall, quick prints.

  4-arch ............. Supports TOUCHING BUILD PLATE ONLY, plus a brim of about 8 mm.
                       88.3 mm tall on 398.5 mm2 of first layer. 12.5 % overhang: the two cheek
                       undersides and the index fan around Z 30-60, the crown hump, and the
                       stepped front pads near the plate. All of it is reachable from the
                       plate, so "touching build plate only" is enough.

NOT IMPORTANT - your defaults are fine
  Layer height, infill density, infill pattern, wall count, speed. These parts carry almost no
  load: the worst sustained stress anywhere is 0.16 MPa, about 0.3 % of PLA's yield. 3 walls
  and 20 % infill is plenty. No need to quote anything special.

QUANTITIES
  One of each object. That is TWO heads (objects 3 and 5) and one of everything else.

ONE FIT THAT MATTERS
  Objects 1 and 2 plug into each other on a rectangular spigot at Z 285. The designed
  clearance is 0.350 mm per side sideways and 0.180 mm per side fore-and-aft - deliberately
  different, and deliberately tight in the fore-aft direction. If your machine runs generously
  on outside dimensions the joint may need a scrape; please do not "help" by scaling anything.

NOT IN THIS PACK - I already have them
  The camera retainer and the pocket shims are the two SHARED plates
  (../cam_retainer/print/camera_retainer_PRINT.stl and
  ../camera_shims/print/camera_shims_PRINT.stl), which I have printed already. One of each
  serves every camera head in the build, and they are not reprinted here: the retainer is the
  same solid to 0.005 mm.

WHAT THIS IS
  An over-the-shoulder camera mast for the rear-left deck corner of a LeKiwi robot (objects 1,
  2 and 3) and a forward camera arch for the PincOpen gripper's wrist (objects 4 and 5). All
  five plates are all-PLA; no metal tube, no drilling and no machining anywhere in the build.
