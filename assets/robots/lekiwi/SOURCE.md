# `lekiwi` — LeKiwi mobile base + SO-101 arm

The upstream robot, exactly as [SIGRobotics-UIUC/LeKiwi](https://github.com/SIGRobotics-UIUC/LeKiwi)
ships it: a three-wheel omni base carrying an SO-101 arm, a front camera and a wrist camera. No
modifications. Provenance and licence: below.

```
robot.json            generated by tools/build/make_robots.py - do not hand-edit
urdf/LeKiwi.urdf      upstream URDF/LeKiwi.urdf
meshes/*.stl          upstream URDF/meshes (45 files, metres URDF / millimetre meshes)
print/**              the upstream 3DPrintMeshes plates a component uses - what you put on a bed
```

## Frames and units

Robot frame, millimetres: **+X right, +Y forward, +Z up**. Ground (wheel contact) at **Z −32.9**,
top deck plate at **Z 57.0**, shoulder-pan axis at **(0, 67.15)**. Root link
`base_plate_layer1-v5`.

The URDF is in **metres** and its meshes are in **millimetres**, and the `<mesh>` elements carry no
`scale` — so link poses must be multiplied by 1000 after FK. `src/core/robot-model.js` and
`tools/sim/armfk.py` both do this; anything new that reads the URDF must too.

## Joint limits

The URDF has **no `<limit>` tags** — every joint is `continuous` or `fixed`. `kinematics.joint_limits_deg`
comes from TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100's `Simulation/SO101/so101_new_calib.urdf`, the only SO-101
description that carries limits, re-mapped onto this URDF's **different joint zero** (LeKiwi's zero is
the folded CAD parking pose). The mapping `q_lekiwi = sign · q_ref + offset` was solved geometrically,
not guessed; `kinematics.joint_limits_source` carries the whole derivation and its residual
(max axis-line offset 5.1 mm, max axis angle 0.87°). Read it before you trust a limit.

## `frames.wrist_flange`

Where an end effector bolts on: **+X** is the `arm_wrist_roll` axis (the stock finger direction),
**+Z** is the negated `arm_gripper` hinge axis, **+Y** = Z × X. The origin puts a gripper's back face
on the wrist-roll output flange. This is the frame `lekiwi-pincopen` attaches PincOpen at, and it is
**approximate along +X** — it was derived from the stock gripper because no CAD of the real adapter
plate was available. Expect a few millimetres there, and say so in anything that depends on it.

## Slots

`gripper` · `front_camera` · `wrist_camera_mount` · `pi_case` — each with the stock option, `none`
and `import`. The stock robot does not have an overhead camera or fingertip options; those live in
the `lekiwi-pincopen` composition.

| here | upstream |

Identical meshes (the three omni wheels, the servos) stay separate files, as upstream ships them; git stores
each once.

The other 24 files of `3DPrintMeshes/` (the Dynamixel, wired, 5 V, EU, `_v2` and `modified_`
variants, the Jetson holder, the webcam and wrist camera mounts with their STEP sources) match no
component and are not copied; they are at the pinned commit.

Files are **copied, not symlinked**, so the package is self-contained and can be zipped and
shipped on its own. Nothing in this folder is edited by the studio; `robot.json` next to it
is this project's own file and refers to these paths.
